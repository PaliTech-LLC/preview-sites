What we do

Six things, done thoroughly.

Pick one or hand us the whole yard — we'll tell you honestly what it needs and what it doesn't.

Lawn maintenance & mowing

Weekly or biweekly mowing with clean edging, trimming, and blow-down. The kind of consistency that keeps a lawn looking cared-for instead of just cut.

Garden design & planting

Plant choices that fit your light, soil, and how much time you actually have — laid out so they look good the day we leave and better each year.

Mulch & bed care

Fresh mulch, defined edges, and weeded beds that hold moisture and read as intentional. The detail that makes a whole yard look finished.

Trimming & cleanups

Hedge and shrub shaping, overgrowth cut back, and full spring and fall cleanups that haul off the mess and reset the yard.

Sod & seeding

New sod for an instant lawn or overseeding to thicken what you've got — graded and prepped so it roots in instead of washing away.

Hardscape & retaining

Paver walkways, patios, and retaining walls that handle the grade and the rain — built level, drained right, and made to last.
