The detail menu

Six ways we make it gleam.

From a maintenance wash to multi-stage paint correction, every package is done by hand at your address — finished to the standard you'd expect from a high-end shop.

Full interior & exterior detail

The complete reset — wheels, paint, glass, carpets, seats and console — inside and out in one visit.

Hand wash & carnauba wax

A gentle two-bucket hand wash followed by a wax that beads water and brings the color back to life.

Paint correction

Multi-stage machine polishing that cuts away swirls, scratches and haze until the panels read like glass.

Ceramic coating

A long-lasting protective layer that locks in the gloss, shrugs off water, dirt and UV for years.

Interior shampoo

Deep-cleaned carpets, mats and upholstery — stains lifted, odors gone, surfaces conditioned and fresh.

Headlight restoration

Cloudy, yellowed lenses sanded clear and sealed — brighter night driving and a sharper-looking front end.
