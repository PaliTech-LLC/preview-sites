The studio

A neighborhood spot on South Grand, finding its rhythm.

Studio Nails & Spa sits among the shops and cafés on South Grand, in the heart of South City. We keep the room calm and the music low, open every tool pouch in front of you, and take the time to get the shape right rather than rush you back out the door.

We're a small team that's grown a lot lately — newer chairs, a tidier color wall, and techs who genuinely care about a clean cuticle and color that lasts. Stop in and see for yourself.
