Our studio

A South Grand studio that's getting better every week.

Studio Nails & Spa sits right on South Grand Boulevard, in the middle of one of South City's most-loved stretches of shops and cafes. We're a small walk-in studio, and we've spent the past year listening hard to our regulars — tightening up our timing, refreshing our tools, and slowing the chairs back down so no one feels rushed.

Stop in for a quick lunch polish or settle in for a full spa pedicure. Either way, the goal is the same: clean work, fair prices, and a calm half hour that's actually yours.
