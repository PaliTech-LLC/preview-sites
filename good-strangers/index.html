Who we are

Named for the best part of a good morning.

Good Strangers started with a simple idea: a counter where the person next to you might be a stranger when you walk in and a familiar face by the time you leave. We're an independent shop on Cherokee Street, run by people who live a few blocks away.

No app, no drive-through, no rush. Just careful coffee, a warm room, and a seat that's yours for as long as you want it.
