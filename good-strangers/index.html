Who we are

The name is the promise.

Good Strangers started with a simple idea — that a café should be the easiest place in the neighborhood to feel welcome, whether it's your first morning here or your five hundredth. We're independent, we're on Cherokee Street, and we pull every shot like someone we'd want to see again.

No app to download, no points to chase. Just careful coffee, a warm room, and a counter where being a stranger only lasts about a minute.

"Come in a stranger, leave a regular."
