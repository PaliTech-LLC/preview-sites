What turns up at Elder's.

We don't keep a fixed catalog — the shop is whatever walked in this week. Here's the kind of thing you'll usually be picking through. The best way to know what's in? Stop by, or give us a call.

Vintage furniture

Mid-century dressers, oak side tables, the odd Victorian piece — solid wood that's already outlived a few owners and will outlast a few more.

Depression & cut glass

Amber, jadeite and milk glass, decanters and pressed-glass dishes — the case fills back up almost as fast as it empties.

Vinyl & the record crates

Soul, jazz, classic rock and St. Louis pressings worth flipping through. Regulars know to check the crates first thing.

Estate jewelry & watches

Costume brooches to the occasional gold piece and wind-up watch. Small, well-priced things that are easy to fall for.

Books, maps & ephemera

Old St. Louis postcards, sheet music, pulp paperbacks and the occasional first edition tucked in the back stacks.

Looking for something specific?

Tell us what you're hunting — a particular maker, an era, a piece to match what you've already got. We turn over stock constantly and we'll keep an eye out. Easiest is a quick call to (314) 772-1436.

On Cherokee Row

A shop built for the dig.

Elder's sits right on Cherokee Antique Row in South City — the stretch of Cherokee Street that's been St. Louis's hunting ground for old and unusual things for generations. We're proud to be one of the doors worth opening on it.

Up to now we've mostly lived on an Etsy storefront, but the real shop is the experience: shelves you can actually browse, prices you can talk over, and stock that changes every single week. Come spend an afternoon — that's the whole idea.
