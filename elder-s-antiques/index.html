What you'll find

A little of everything, gone over by hand.

No two visits are the same, but here's the kind of thing that tends to fill the floor. Stock turns over — come dig, or call ahead and ask.

01

Vintage & antique furniture

Dressers, side tables, chairs and the occasional sideboard worth rearranging the living room for — solid wood, real age, honest wear.

02

Glassware, china & barware

Depression glass, mismatched stemware, old coffee tins and the kind of cocktail set that makes a shelf look lived-in.

03

Lighting & lamps

Table lamps, hanging fixtures and shades from a few different eras — rewired and tested when we can, sold as-is when they're too good to pass up.

04

Vintage prints, frames & art

Old advertising, framed prints, mirrors and the odd original — wall-fillers and conversation pieces in roughly equal measure.

05

Collectibles & oddities

Records, old signage, costume jewelry, tins, toys and the genuinely strange — the shelf people end up spending the longest in front of.

06

Looking for something specific?

Tell us what you're hunting — a particular maker, a missing piece of a set, a style for a room. We'll keep an eye out and give you a call if it comes through the door.

The shop

A fixture on Cherokee Antique Row.

Elder's Antiques sits in the middle of one of St. Louis' great rummaging streets — the stretch of Cherokee Street where antique hunters have been wandering shop to shop for generations. We've kept our doors open the old-fashioned way: a floor packed with real pieces, fair prices on the tag, and time to talk if you want to know where something came from.

Right now you'll mostly find us on Etsy — this page is a preview of a proper home for the shop. Either way, the best of it never makes it online. For that, you walk in.
