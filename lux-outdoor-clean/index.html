What we clean

Six ways we make the glass disappear.

From the windows you see every day to the skylights you forget about, every surface gets the same patient, streak-free attention.

Our signature

Interior & exterior windows

Both sides of every window, hand-finished and buffed to a clear, even shine — no streaks, no haze, no film left at the corners. The work most people notice first.

Screen cleaning

Screens removed, washed free of pollen and grime, dried and reset so the view stays as clear as the glass.

Track & sill detailing

The grit and cobwebs in the tracks and sills, brushed and wiped out — the detail that makes a window look truly clean.

Gutter cleaning

Leaves and debris cleared from gutters and downspouts so water runs where it should — protecting fascia and foundation.

Skylights & hard-to-reach glass

High windows, skylights and stairwell panes reached safely with the right gear — the spots a ladder alone can't manage.

Storefront & commercial glass

Entrances and display glass kept spotless on a schedule that suits your hours — a clear first impression for every customer.

Local & meticulous

A small crew that treats your home like its own.

LUX Outdoor Clean is owner-run out of Brentwood, and we show up in our own marked truck — the one you see right here. No subcontractors, no rushing through the corners.

We work clean, protect your sills and landscaping, and don't leave until every pane is clear from the inside and the out. If something isn't right, we make it right before we pack up.
