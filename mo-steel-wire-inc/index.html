What we build

Six lines of work, one shop.

We fabricate and supply the metal, and our crew installs it — so the fence or railing you get is the one we made.

Chain-link fencing

Galvanized and vinyl-coated chain-link for yards, lots and job sites — set on concrete-footed posts that hold true.

Ornamental steel

Welded steel pickets and panels with the strength of iron and a clean, finished face for homes and storefronts.

Custom gates

Swing and rolling gates built to your opening — sized, hung and latched so they swing square for years.

Railings & handrails

Steel stair and deck railings fabricated to code, ground smooth and primed to keep hands and steps safe.

Wire fabrication

Custom wire mesh, guards and panels cut and welded to spec — the work most fence suppliers send out, we do in-house.

Commercial & security fencing

Perimeter and security fence for warehouses, yards and contractors — heavier gauge, taller runs, and gates rated for daily traffic. Tell us the site and we'll spec the line.

Why fabrication matters

We make the metal — not just resell it.

Because the cutting, welding and finishing happen here, your gate fits its opening, your railing meets the run, and a custom piece isn't a special order — it's just Tuesday. One crew owns the job from the drawing to the last post.
