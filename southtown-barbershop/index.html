The shop

A real neighborhood barbershop.

Southtown has been cutting Dogtown's hair the honest way — take your time, get the line right, talk a little ball. No apps, no fuss. Just a good chair, a sharp cut, and a shop that feels like the block it sits on.

Come as you are. First-timers leave regulars.

"Sit down, get it right, send you out sharp — that's the whole job."
