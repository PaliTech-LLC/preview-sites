What we build

Six kinds of fence, one standard of work.

Every line is laid out, dug, and set in person. We pick lumber that weathers well and finish the details most crews skip.

Wood privacy fences

Solid cedar and treated pine privacy fencing — tight boards, capped tops, and posts set deep so the line stays true. Our most-requested build.

Picket fences

Classic front-yard pickets with crisp spacing and a clean curb look the whole street notices.

Ranch & split-rail

Open rail fencing for acreage and corner lots — wide spans, clean horizontals, room to breathe.

Gates & access

Matched swing and double gates that hang square, latch clean, and don't sag two summers later.

Repairs & replacement

Leaning posts, storm damage, a few bad sections — we reset what's worth saving and replace what isn't.

Staining & finishing

Seal a new fence or bring an older one back — a finish coat that protects the wood and evens the tone.
