Our corner of Cherokee

A SHOP THAT KNOWS YOUR NAME

Catracho — Honduran, and proud of it.

Catrachos Cutz sits on Cherokee Street, in a stretch of South City where the neighborhood still feels like one. We named the shop after where we're from, and we cut for everyone who walks through the door — the same care for the first-timer as for the regular who's been in our chair for years.

Come for the fade, stay for the conversation. Walk in when you've got a minute, or call ahead and we'll hold a chair for you.
