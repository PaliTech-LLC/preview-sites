The shop on Jefferson

Independent, by people who love putting an outfit together.

City Boutique Stl is a small, independent women's clothing shop in South City St. Louis. We hand-pick what comes through the door — a few of this, a couple of that — so the floor stays fresh and you almost never run into someone wearing the same thing.

Stop in, try things on, and take your time. We'd rather you leave with one piece you love than an armful you're not sure about.
