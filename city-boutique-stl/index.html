The shop

Small rack, big personality.

City Boutique Stl is a one-of-a-kind women's clothing shop tucked into the Cherokee St / South Grand corridor in South City. We are small and newer — and we like it that way. It means we can buy in tiny runs, chase the pieces we actually love, and tell you honestly what's worth it.

Walk in, take your time on the rack, try things on, and leave with something that feels like you and not like the mall.

"We'd rather carry ten things we love than a hundred we don't."
