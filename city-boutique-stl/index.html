The shop

Independently owned, on Jefferson Ave.

City Boutique Stl is a small women's clothing shop in South City — the kind of place where the owner picks every piece herself and the rack looks different than it did last week. We keep it curated, not crowded.

There's no online store and no endless catalog; the fun is walking in, flipping through, and finding the one thing that feels like you. If you spotted something here last visit and it's gone, that's the idea — it probably found its person.

What you'll find

A little of everything, none of it twice.

No appointments, no membership, no checkout cart — just a walk-in shop with a rack worth stopping for. Here's how the visit usually goes.

01

Everyday & dressed-up pieces

Tops, dresses, denim, and layering pieces for real life in the city — picked to mix with what's already in your closet.

02

New arrivals, most weeks

The rack turns over often, so there's almost always something you didn't see last time. Follow along or just stop in to catch the drop.

03

One-of-one finds

We buy in small runs, so most styles come in just a piece or two. If it fits and you love it, it's likely meant to be.

04

Honest styling help

Tell us where you're headed and we'll pull a few things that work together — no pressure, no pushing the priciest rack.

05

Accessories & little extras

The finishing touches — bags, jewelry, and the kind of small pieces that pull a whole look together.

06

A South City stop worth making

Right on Jefferson Ave — easy to pair with a coffee and a stroll. Walk in, take your time, leave with something good.
