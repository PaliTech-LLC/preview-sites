What we do

Import work, done properly

Six things imported and foreign vehicles need most — handled with the right diagnostics, the right parts, and no upsell.

Computer diagnostics

Factory-level scanning for European and Asian systems — we read the codes most shops can't, then explain in plain English what's actually wrong before we touch a thing.

Brakes & rotors

Pads, rotors, calipers and fluid spec'd to your make. Quiet, even-stopping brakes — with the sensor and wear systems reset the way the manufacturer intended.

Timing belts & chains

The interval that protects your engine. We replace belts, chains, tensioners and water pumps on schedule for makes where a missed timing job means a new motor.

Oil & fluid service

Full-synthetic oil, coolant, transmission and brake fluids matched exactly to your import's specification — never a generic "one-size" fill that voids your wear schedule.

Electrical & sensors

Modules, sensors, batteries and wiring gremlins on complex European electronics — traced to the real fault instead of replacing parts until the light goes out.

Pre-purchase inspections

Thinking of buying a used import? Bring it in first. A thorough top-to-bottom check that tells you what you're really getting — and what it'll cost down the road.
