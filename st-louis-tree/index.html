What we do

Six ways we look after your trees

Every job starts with an honest look at the tree and what it needs — then the right work, done safely, with the yard left tidy.

Tree removal

Hazardous, dead, or in the way — we take down trees of any size with rigging and care, protecting the house, fence, and beds below. The stump and debris go too.

Trimming & pruning

Shape, thin, and lift the canopy so your tree grows strong and stays clear of the roof and lines.

Stump grinding

We grind old stumps well below grade so you reclaim the spot for grass, beds, or a clean walk-through — no trip hazard left behind.

Storm & emergency cleanup

A limb on the roof or a tree across the drive can't wait. We respond fast, make it safe, and clear the mess so your day gets back on track.

Deadwooding

We remove the dead and dying branches that drop without warning — safer for people below, healthier for the tree above.

Health & disease assessment

Worried a tree is sick or unstable? We read the signs — fungus, dieback, root and lean issues — and tell you straight whether it can be saved or should come down, before it becomes an emergency.

Local hands, real tree knowledge

St. Louis Tree Co. is a tree service rooted right here in the St. Louis area. We're the crew neighbors call when a tree looks risky, when a storm leaves a mess, or when a yard just needs a careful hand — and we show up, look closely, and tell you straight what the tree needs.

No upselling a removal when a prune will do, and no leaving your lawn torn up when we're done. Just safe, tidy work and a fair, free estimate before anything starts.

Why careful tree work matters

A tree is heavy, alive, and often closer to your roof and power than it looks. Done wrong, the cut goes the wrong way; done right, the risk simply disappears. That's the whole job.

  • We protect what's below. Roof, fence, garden beds, and the neighbor's yard — rigged and lowered, not dropped.
  • We leave it clean. Brush hauled, chips raked, stump ground — the yard looks cared for, not cut up.
  • We tell you the truth. If a tree can be saved, we say so. If it has to come down, you'll know why.
