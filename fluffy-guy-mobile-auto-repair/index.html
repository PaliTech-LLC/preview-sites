What we fix roadside

Real repairs, brought to your car

From a dead battery in the cold to a brake job before a road trip — here's the work we handle without you ever leaving your vehicle.

Roadside diagnostics

Check-engine light or a noise you can't place? We scan the codes on-site and tell you straight what's actually wrong.

Brakes & rotors

Grinding, squealing, or a soft pedal — pads and rotors swapped where you're parked so you stop short of the next light.

Batteries, alternators & starters

Won't crank or dies overnight? We test the charging system and replace the failed part right there — no jump-and-pray.

Belts & hoses

A snapped serpentine belt or a leaking hose can strand you fast. We carry the common sizes and get the loop spinning again.

No-start & won't-crank

Turn the key and nothing? We trace it from the battery to the ignition and fuel system until the engine fires.

Pre-purchase inspections

Buying used? We meet you and the seller, go over the car top to bottom, and tell you whether it's worth the asking price.

Who's the Fluffy Guy?

A mechanic who actually shows up

Fluffy Guy Mobile Auto Repair started with a simple idea: most breakdowns don't happen during business hours, and nobody enjoys waiting in a lobby for a quote. So we put the shop on wheels and kept the phone on around the clock.

Based out of Gravois Ave in St. Louis, we drive to your car — home, work, or the side of I-44 — and do the job where it sits. The name's memorable on purpose; the work speaks for itself.
