What we fix

Repairs, diagnosis & recovery

Six things go wrong with a pool more than anything else. We carry the parts and know the equipment, so most calls are diagnosed and back in the water without a second trip.

/ 01

Pump & filter repair

Noisy, leaking, or won't prime? We rebuild and replace pumps, motors, and filter systems so circulation runs quiet and clean again.

/ 02

Leak detection & patch

Losing water and not sure why? We pressure-test plumbing and the shell, pinpoint the leak, and patch it before it undermines the deck.

/ 03

Liner repair & replacement

Tears, wrinkles, and faded vinyl get patched or fully replaced with a clean fit — measured, ordered, and installed by the same crew.

/ 04

Heater service

Cold water in season is a fixable problem. We service and repair gas and heat-pump units so the swim season actually lasts the season.

/ 05

Green-to-clean recovery

Opened the cover to a swamp? We balance, treat, and clear neglected or storm-hit water back to swimmable — usually within days.

/ 06

Seasonal open & close

Spring openings and winter closings done properly — equipment checked, lines blown, and the pool protected so next season starts clean.
