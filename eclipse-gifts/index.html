About the shop

A small shop on Gravois with a big sense of fun.

Eclipse Gifts sits on Gravois Avenue in South City, and we run it the way we'd want our own corner store to feel — friendly, a little surprising, and never the same two months running. We buy in small, hunt for things you won't find in a big-box aisle, and keep the mix moving.

Come in to pick up a gift, or just to see what's new. There's almost always something on the shelf that wasn't there last week.
