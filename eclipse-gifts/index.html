What we carry

A little of everything, none of it filler.

From the last-minute birthday card to the gift you've been planning for weeks — here's what you'll find when you walk in.

Cards & Stationery

Letterpress, funny, and heartfelt — a wall of cards for the occasion you forgot until this morning, plus notebooks and pens worth keeping.

Candles & Home Scents

Hand-poured soy candles, incense, and room mists from makers a few zip codes away — the smell that makes a small apartment feel cared for.

Jewelry & Accessories

Everyday earrings, delicate chains, and a few statement pieces — most of it handmade, all of it priced so you can actually gift it.

From Local Makers

Ceramics, prints, soaps, and small-batch goods from St. Louis artists — buy a gift and keep a neighbor's craft going at the same time.

Free Gift Wrapping

Pick anything off the shelf and we'll wrap it at the counter — paper, ribbon, and a handwritten tag, no charge, while you wait.

Help Picking the One

Tell us who it's for and the budget — we genuinely like the puzzle of finding the perfect gift, and we're good at it.

Our corner of Gravois

A neighborhood shop with a good eye.

Eclipse Gifts started from a simple idea — South City deserved a place to find a thoughtful, beautifully wrapped gift without driving to a mall. We hand-pick everything on the shelves and lean hard on St. Louis makers.

Come in to browse, ask for a recommendation, or just smell every candle on the table. There's no pressure here — only the quiet satisfaction of leaving with exactly the right thing.
