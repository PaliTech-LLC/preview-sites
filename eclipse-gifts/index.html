A look at what's in right now

Always changing, always hand-picked.

We don't keep a fixed catalog — the shelves shift with the season and with what local makers bring us. Here's the kind of thing you'll usually find when you walk in.

01

Greeting cards & paper

A wall of cards for every occasion and a few for no occasion at all — funny, heartfelt, and the kind you can't find at the drugstore.

02

Candles & home scents

Small-batch candles, incense, and room sprays in scents we actually burn ourselves — easy to gift, hard to leave behind.

03

Local maker shelf

Ceramics, jewelry, prints, and goods from St. Louis artists and small makers — the rotating heart of the shop, and what people come back for.

04

Little luxuries

Stationery, enamel pins, lip balms, tea, and the small treat-yourself things that make a quick stop turn into a basket.

05

Free gift wrapping

Tell us it's a gift and we'll wrap it at the counter while you wait — paper, ribbon, and a card tucked in, no extra charge.

06

Help finding the one

Stuck on what to get? Tell us who it's for and your budget — we love the puzzle, and we'll walk the shop with you.

The shop on Gravois

Small shop, big soft spot for the perfect find.

Eclipse Gifts is an independent shop on Gravois Avenue in South City St. Louis, stocked the way we'd shop ourselves — cards and candles up front, a rotating shelf of local makers in back, and someone behind the counter who actually wants to help you find the right thing.

Because the inventory turns over so often, every visit is a little different. Come in for one card, leave with the gift you didn't know you were looking for.
