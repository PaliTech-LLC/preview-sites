The shop

Run by two people who actually live on Cherokee.

Unc + TT's Place is a small, independent boutique at 2610 Cherokee Street — part of the stretch of South City shops, art and food that gives the neighborhood its name. We're new, we're proud of it, and we'd rather have one honest rack of things we love than a warehouse of filler.

Stop in, talk to us, dig through the racks. There's no catalog to memorize and no two weeks look the same — that's kind of the whole point.
