Who we are

A little corner of Cherokee Street that’s all ours.

Unc + TT’s Place is a small, independent boutique in South City St. Louis. We hunt, we curate, and we put out finds we’d actually keep for ourselves — vintage apparel, accessories, and the occasional why-not piece.

There’s no big catalog and no two weeks alike. That’s the point: stop in, see what landed, and walk out with something nobody else on the block is wearing.
