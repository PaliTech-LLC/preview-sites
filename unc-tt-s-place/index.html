Who’s behind it

Two names on the door, one block we love.

Unc + TT’s Place is a brand-new, family-run shop on Cherokee Street — the most colorful, independent stretch in South City. We opened because the neighborhood deserved a spot that feels like us: friendly, curated, and a little bit different.

Right now we live on Instagram and on the block. Come in, talk to us, and tell us what you’re hunting for — that’s the whole point.
