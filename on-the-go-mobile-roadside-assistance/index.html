What we handle

Six roadside fixes, one phone call

If your car won't go, we make it go — or we move it. Here's what we get called out for most.

Battery jump-starts

Turn the key and nothing? We bring the boost, get you running, and make sure the alternator's actually holding a charge before we leave.

Tire changes

Flat on the shoulder? We swap on your spare safely and get you rolling — no jack, no lug wrench, no problem.

Car lockouts

Keys locked inside — engine running or not? We pop the door without scratching the paint and have you back in within minutes.

Fuel delivery

Ran the tank dry? We bring enough gas to get you to the nearest station — no long walk down the highway with a can.

Winch-outs & recovery

Slid off into a ditch, stuck in mud or snow? We winch you back onto solid ground and check you're safe to drive on.

Light towing

When a roadside fix isn't enough, we'll hook up your car or light truck and tow it to your shop, your driveway, or wherever you need it — without the runaround of a big tow yard.

Why On The Go

A neighbor with a truck, not a call center

On The Go Mobile Roadside Assistance is run out of Hazelwood, and we treat every call like it's a family member stranded on the side of the road. You talk to the person who's actually coming — no ticket numbers, no being put on hold while your hazards blink.

We come to you wherever you're stuck, day or night, and we don't leave until you're moving again or safely on the way to where you need to be.
