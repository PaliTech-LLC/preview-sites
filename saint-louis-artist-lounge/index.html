An artist lounge first, a gallery second — pull up a chair and stay a while.

Tucked into a studio building off Ohio Avenue in South City, the Lounge is where neighborhood painters, printmakers, and photographers hang their newest work and actually hang around to talk about it. There's no velvet rope and no whisper-quiet hush.

Shows rotate every few weeks, openings are open to everyone, and just about everything on the wall is for sale — usually straight from the person who made it.

On the walls

A look around the room.

Rotating work from St. Louis artists — paintings, prints, photography, and the occasional sculpture. What's up changes often, so it's worth stopping back.

What's inside

More than a wall of paintings.

The Lounge is a working space as much as a showroom. Here's what you'll find when you stop by.

01

Rotating exhibitions

A new show every few weeks — solo features and group hangs of local painters, printmakers, and photographers. Always free to come in and look.

02

Opening receptions

Meet-the-artist nights with the maker right there in the room. Casual, conversational, and open to the whole neighborhood — bring a friend.

03

Original art for sale

Most of the wall is available — originals and prints at prices made for real homes, not just collectors. We'll happily connect you straight with the artist.

04

Open studio & lounge

The reason for the name — a relaxed back room where artists work, swap ideas, and visitors are welcome to sit, sketch, or just take it all in.

05

Workshops & demos

Hands-on sessions led by the artists who show here — small groups, all levels, the kind of evening you leave with paint on your hands.

06

Show your own work

An open call for South City makers looking for wall space and a friendly first audience. Stop in or call and we'll talk about the next opening.

Who we are

A South City corner for the people who make things.

The Saint Louis Artist Lounge grew out of a simple idea: give neighborhood artists a real wall to show on and a warm room to gather in, without the gatekeeping of a downtown gallery. It's run by and for the local creative community on Ohio Avenue.

Come to see a show, leave with a piece, or just spend an hour somewhere that feels like St. Louis — you'll always be welcome to walk in.
