What happens here

More than a wall to hang on.

The Lounge is part gallery, part living room for the South City art scene. Here's what you'll find when you stop in.

Rotating local exhibitions

A new group or solo show every few weeks, hung salon-style — always free to walk through. Most pieces on the walls are for sale, and you're buying straight from the artist.

Opening receptions

First-Friday-style openings where you can meet the artists, talk shop, and bring a friend.

Studio & work nights

Open-table evenings to draw, paint, or finish a piece alongside other makers from the block.

Small workshops

Hands-on sessions led by neighborhood artists — printmaking, watercolor, and the occasional one-off.

Wall space for artists

Showing local makers who don't have a gallery yet. If you've got work and want a wall, come talk to us.

A community living room

Mostly, it's a place to sit with a coffee and good company surrounded by work made down the street. No ticket, no hush — just South City's art scene with the door propped open.

Who we are

A lounge first, a gallery second.

Saint Louis Artist Lounge grew out of a simple idea: South City makes a staggering amount of art, and most of it never gets a wall. So we built one — a warm room on Ohio Ave where neighbors can wander in, see what local hands are making, and stay a while.

No velvet ropes, no "please don't touch the conversation." Just artists, their work, and the people who love both — all in one comfortable room.
