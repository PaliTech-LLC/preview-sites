A fence crew that treats your yard like their own.

What we build

Fencing for the whole property.

Every fence is built to the line, the slope, and the reason you want it — privacy, pets, a pool code, or plain good looks.

Wood privacy fences

Cedar and treated-pine privacy walls, board-on-board or shadowbox, set on posts poured deep enough to stay plumb through Missouri freezes.

Vinyl & composite

Low-maintenance vinyl that never needs paint or stain — clean white or neutral tones that hold up to sun and rain for the long haul.

Ornamental aluminum & steel

Powder-coated picket fencing with a classic wrought-iron look — pool-code compliant, rust-resistant, and built for curb appeal.

Chain-link & security

Galvanized and black-vinyl-coated chain-link for yards, pets, and commercial lots — dependable, affordable, and quick to install.

Gates & openers

Matching walk-gates, double-drive gates, and automatic openers — squared, latched, and hung so they swing true years from now.

Repairs & post resets

Storm damage, leaning posts, sagging gates, or a few rotten boards — we fix what you have instead of selling you a fence you don't need.
